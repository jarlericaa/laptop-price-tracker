Description

Stay productive wherever you go with the HP 15-fc0933AU Laptop (Natural Silver).

Designed for everyday work, study, and entertainment, this laptop features a 15.6-inch Full HD IPS display that delivers crisp visuals and vibrant colors for an enjoyable viewing experience. Powered by the AMD Ryzen™ 3 7320U processor, 8GB RAM, and a 512GB SSD, it provides responsive performance for multitasking, browsing, document editing, online meetings, and streaming.

With AMD Radeon™ Graphics, Windows 11 Home, and Microsoft Office Home 2019 pre-installed, you're ready to be productive from day one. It also includes Microsoft 365 Basic for added cloud storage and productivity features, plus an HP Everyday 16-inch Laptop Briefcase to keep your device protected while you're on the move.
